### Corections 
1. **Sentence Structure and Tenses:**

    - “At 9h aproximataly I disk to gaz service” can be rephrased as “Around 9:00 am, I went to the gas service.”

    - “Until to Leticia wake up” should be “Until Leticia woke up.”
